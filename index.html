What superintelligence (SI) is

Artificial intelligence (AI) today helps with tasks: drafting, summarizing, spotting patterns. Superintelligence (SI) describes AI that would beat the best people at nearly every kind of thinking, not just one task.

SI is a name for where AI is heading. It is not a claim that it has arrived.

2 of 6

Why SI is the new AI

The word “AI” now covers everything from autocomplete to systems that act on their own. We use SI as the name for the field as it grows up: the same news, under a newer and clearer name. You will still hear AI everywhere, so we explain both.

Same field. Newer name. We always spell out both.

3 of 6

Fact vs rumor

  • ConfirmedThe source said it itself: an official post, a filing, a published paper.
  • ReportedCredible outlets say so, but the source has not confirmed it.
  • Still openA claim we are still checking. It never becomes advice.

A label tells you who said it, not whether it is good news.

4 of 6

Try it: label three claims

A lab publishes a paper about its own new model, on its own site.

Two outlets, citing people familiar with the plans, say a company will release a model next month. The company has not commented.

One anonymous post says a model has reached superintelligence.

5 of 6

How to use it at work

  1. Start small. Pick one low-risk task a week and try it.
  2. Ask for a draft, not a decision. Keep the judgement yourself.
  3. Check what matters. Open the source before you act or share.

Small, checked and repeated beats big and rushed.
